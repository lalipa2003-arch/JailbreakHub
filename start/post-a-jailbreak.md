## 🤝 Community Contributions

Got a functional jailbreak? We want to feature it! Community-submitted prompts are tested and archived directly in the [`jb-community/`](./jb-community) directory with full credit to the researcher.

### 🎯 Submission Checklist
To get your prompt added, navigate to the **Issues** tab, click **New Issue**, and provide the following:

| Requirement | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Target Model** | Specify the exact AI model or version family (e.g., `GPT-5.2`, `Claude 5.5 Sonnet`). |
| **The jailbreak** | Paste your raw, unmodified jailbreak prompt text. |
| **Proof of Concept** | Provide a text log or **paste a screenshot** showing the successful guardrail bypass (just press `Ctrl+V` / `Cmd+V` in the box). |
| **Credit Name** | Let us know your preferred name or social handle (e.g., X, GitHub) for credid and authorship, or specify *anonymous*. |

Then i approve it or nah.
